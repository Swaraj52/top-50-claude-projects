What happens when you ask

  1. 1
    Understand. Filters are removed from the question, quotes become phrases, and filler words are dropped. The understanding is shown on the results.
  2. 2
    Retrieve. The same local ranking as search picks up to eight items. Title and tag matches weigh more than body matches.
  3. 3
    Quote. One sentence per item is chosen by term overlap, then quoted with its title. Nothing is invented; if no passage matches, the answer says so.
